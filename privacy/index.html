Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

ndungu.dev is a static personal and consulting site published by David Ndungu. It does not run its own servers, does not have user accounts, and does not host a form that stores your data on this domain. This page explains the third-party tools embedded on the site and what they collect.

Analytics

The site loads Google Analytics (gtag.js) to measure page views and general traffic patterns. Google may set cookies and collect standard analytics data such as approximate location, device type, and referring page. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google processes this data.

Embedded third-party components

The homepage hero loads a 3D scene from Spline (splinetool/spline-viewer), served from Spline's own domains. Loading that component means your browser makes a request directly to Spline's infrastructure, subject to Spline's privacy policy. Google Fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com to render the site's typeface.

What happens when you contact me

Booking a call routes to Cal.com, and email/phone links open your own mail or phone app — none of that is intercepted or stored by this site. Any information you send me directly (by email, phone, or the booking form) is used only to respond to you and, if you become a client, to deliver the work. I don't sell or share it with third parties for marketing.

No advertising or tracking pixels beyond analytics

This site does not run ad networks, retargeting pixels, or third-party trackers beyond the Google Analytics tag described above.

Questions

For any privacy question or a request to see or delete data I hold about you, email david@sire.run.
